UV upturn in Virgo-cluster galaxies

Which quiescent early-type galaxies show an ultraviolet upturn, and what stellar populations can account for it?

Scientific question

Which quiescent early-type galaxies show an ultraviolet upturn, and what stellar populations can account for it?

Work carried out

  • Multiwavelength photometric selection of candidate UV-upturn galaxies.
  • Spectral energy distribution fitting using CIGALE.
  • Comparison of stellar masses, star formation rates and metallicity indicators.

Key findings

  • The study identifies and confirms UV-upturn candidates using SED fits.
  • The inferred hot stellar component has temperatures of about 13,000–18,000 K.
  • The results support evolved low-mass stars, including extreme horizontal-branch populations, as a plausible source of the UV excess.
