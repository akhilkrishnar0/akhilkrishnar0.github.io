How do the outer ring and globular cluster system constrain the interaction history of NGC 4262?

Scientific question

How do the outer ring and globular cluster system constrain the interaction history of NGC 4262?

Work carried out

  • Deep CFHT/NGVS optical imaging and HST archival observations.
  • Surface photometry, globular-cluster candidate selection and colour analysis.
  • UV/optical comparisons of ring and host regions in the 2025 study.

Key findings

  • The 2024 paper estimates 266 ± 16 globular clusters and specific frequency 4.2 ± 0.8.
  • A measured globular-cluster colour gradient of −0.05 ± 0.01 inside 5.5 arcmin is reported.
  • The 2025 comparative work reports relatively weak ring star formation and places the galaxy in the green valley.
