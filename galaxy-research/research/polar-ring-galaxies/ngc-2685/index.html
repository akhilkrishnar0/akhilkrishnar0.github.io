NGC 2685

What do the stellar populations and resolved UV emission reveal about the ring and host components of this polar structure?

Scientific question

What do the stellar populations and resolved UV emission reveal about the ring and host components of this polar structure?

Work carried out

  • AstroSat/UVIT FUV/NUV and archival optical imaging.
  • Decomposed SED fitting of host and ring regions.
  • Resolved comparisons across three nearby peculiar systems.

Key findings

  • The 2025 paper finds intermediate host/ring trends relative to NGC 3718 and NGC 4262.
  • Ring star formation is stronger than in NGC 4262 within the studied sample.
  • The paper places NGC 2685 at an intermediate stage in its proposed observational sequence, not a demonstrated universal timeline.
