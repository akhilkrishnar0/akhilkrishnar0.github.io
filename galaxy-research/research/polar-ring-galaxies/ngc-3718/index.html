Warped disc and star formation

NGC 3718

How does the warped, gas-rich structure relate to recent star formation and the stellar population of the host?

Scientific question

How does the warped, gas-rich structure relate to recent star formation and the stellar population of the host?

Work carried out

  • AstroSat/UVIT and archival optical–infrared imaging.
  • Surface photometry and spatially separated host/ring SED fits with CIGALE.
  • Resolved star-forming regions and published H I/CO context.

Key findings

  • The ring regions show stronger recent star formation than the host in the comparative study.
  • Approximately 72% of the identified star-forming regions lie in the ring structure.
  • The 2025 paper interprets this system as an early stage in a proposed sequence; this remains a model to test with larger samples.
