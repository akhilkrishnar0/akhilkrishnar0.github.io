DES J024008.08−551047.5 (DJ0240)

Can the morphology and photometry distinguish this object from other ring-galaxy types?

Scientific question

Can the morphology and photometry distinguish this object from other ring-galaxy types?

Work carried out

  • DECaLS g, r and z imaging.
  • Isophotal position-angle and colour measurements.
  • Two-dimensional GALFIT structural decomposition.

Key findings

  • The projected position angles of host and ring are approximately 10° and 80°, respectively.
  • The ring is about three times more extended than the host and is bluer in g−r (0.59 ± 0.10 versus 0.86 ± 0.02 mag).
  • The paper classifies DJ0240 as a promising polar-ring candidate; spectroscopic kinematic confirmation is still needed.
