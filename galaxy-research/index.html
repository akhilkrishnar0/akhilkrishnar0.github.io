Galaxy structure and evolution

Why does the Universe produce such diverse galaxy structures?

Galaxies exhibit a wide diversity of structures, including spiral arms, bars, rings, warped discs, and diffuse stellar components. These structures develop through gravitational dynamics, gas accretion, interactions and star formation. Understanding their formation and evolution is a central problem in galaxy evolution.

NASA, ESA, H. Teplitz (Caltech/IPAC, Caltech), M. Rafelski (Caltech/IPAC, Caltech), Anton Koekemoer (STScI), Rogier Windhorst (ASU), Zoltan Levay (STScI)

My research investigates galaxy structure and evolution using multiwavelength observations, stellar populations and numerical modelling, with emphasis on polar-ring galaxies, low-surface-brightness systems and disc dynamics.
