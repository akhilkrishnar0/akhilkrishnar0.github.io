Biography

Akhil Krishna R. is a postdoctoral fellow at the Indian Institute of Astrophysics, Bengaluru, working on galaxy structure and evolution.

Research

Polar-ring galaxies, ultraviolet stellar populations, globular cluster systems, giant low-surface-brightness galaxies, star formation and disc dynamics. My work combines multiwavelength imaging, photometric analysis, modelling and simulations.

Collaborations and memberships

WALLABY collaboration member; life member of the Astronomical Society of India. Research co-authorships span galaxy evolution, star formation and stellar astrophysics.

Teaching and mentoring

Mentored five M.Sc. thesis students. Teaching experience in introductory astronomy, Python programming and astronomy laboratory courses. Resource person for astronomy workshops.

Observational programmes

  • AstroSat/UVIT — awarded time across four proposal cycles (A11, A12, A14, T05).
  • 3.6-m Devasthal Optical Telescope / ADFOSC — Director’s Discretionary Time.
  • ESO New Technology Telescope / EFOSC2 — Period 113 observing time.

Academic service

Editorial Assistant, Mapana Journal of Sciences (2021–present); local organizing committees for YAM 2024, a 2023 national astrophysics seminar and ASI 2019. Workshop resource person, 2025.
