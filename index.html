PROFILE

About

My research uses multiwavelength imaging, surface photometry, structural decomposition, and stellar-population analysis to investigate the formation and evolution of galaxies.

Research interests include polar-ring galaxies, low-surface-brightness systems, stellar bars, and disk dynamics.
