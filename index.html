Research

I am interested in the formation, structure, and evolution of galaxies. My work combines observations at different wavelengths with photometric analysis, dynamical modelling, and simulations.

My research includes:

  • Understanding how galaxies acquire and transform their stellar and gaseous structures through internal processes and interactions.
  • Studying star formation and stellar populations across different galactic environments, including low-density regions of disks.
  • Investigating how the distribution and motion of baryonic matter relate to galaxy dynamics and dark matter.
  • Connecting observational measurements with numerical models to understand galaxy evolution.
