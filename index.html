Research

My research focuses on galaxy formation and evolution, particularly the relationships between galaxy structure, star formation, and dynamics. I use ultraviolet and optical observations, surface photometry, stellar-population modelling, and gas kinematics.

My work includes polar-ring galaxies, low-surface-brightness systems, and the structure of disk galaxies.
