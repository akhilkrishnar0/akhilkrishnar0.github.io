MOON ·  01 / RESEARCH

Reading the history written in galaxies.

My research focuses on the formation, structure, and evolution of galaxies — particularly the faint and unusual features that preserve evidence of their past.

01

Polar ring galaxies

Misaligned stellar and gaseous structures and the processes that create them.

02

Low-surface-brightness galaxies

Diffuse stellar disks, ultraviolet light, and structure at the limits of detection.

03

Stellar bars & secular evolution

How bars reshape galaxies — and how we may recognise the signatures they leave behind.

SUN ·  02 / METHODS

Connecting observations to physics.

What appears absent in a galaxy image may simply lie below a survey's detection limits. I am interested in methods that help us distinguish the two.

01
Multi-wavelength astronomy

Combining optical and ultraviolet imaging with other tracers to understand galaxy structure and stellar populations.

02
Faint-feature detection

Image decomposition, wavelets, and low-surface-brightness techniques for finding structures hidden in the noise.

03
Observations & simulations

Comparing galaxies on equal footing by accounting for resolution, sensitivity, and selection effects.
